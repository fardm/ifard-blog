## برای آشنایی با ابسیدین

<br> 

```base
views:
  - type: cards
    name: view
    filters:
      and:
        - or:
            - permalink == "obsidian-review"
            - permalink == "obsidian-install"
    sort:
      - property: file.name
        direction: DESC
    image: note.image
    cardSize: 300
    imageAspectRatio: 0.5

```
